## 2. Satellite broadband and direct-to-device connectivity
- **CONFIRMED / regulation + technology.** FCC published in the 8 September Federal Register an NPRM (ET Docket 26-169; FCC 26-51) to let Part 15 unlicensed devices communicate with US-licensed or market-access satellites on a non-interference basis in 2400–2483.5 MHz and 5725–5850 MHz, and to clarify Part 15 equipment aboard spacecraft. No power or gain increase proposed. Comments 9 November; replies 7 December. Why it matters: this is an industry-wide D2D framework for IoT/handset-class radios, not a single-operator grant. https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/09/08/2026-18282/unleashing-unlicensed-spectrum-for-direct-to-device
- **CONFIRMED / regulation + capital.** FCC Chair Brendan Carr on 8 September circulated a satellite “spectrum abundance” item for the September Open Meeting that would unlock more than 1,000 MHz in the 12.7 GHz and 42 GHz bands for home broadband, in-flight and maritime connectivity, and gateway routing, plus an FNPRM exploring 1,450 MHz more in Ku/Ka and 138.25 GHz in D-band. Why it matters: capacity for Amazon Leo, Starlink, OneWeb and gateways is being written in spectrum rules, not launch manifests. Vote later this month; not yet an adopted R&O. https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DOC-424775A1.txt
- **REPORTED / regulation + competitors.** India’s Digital Communications Commission approved most TRAI satcom-spectrum recommendations: 5% AGR usage charge (4% in notified rural/remote areas), five-year assignment with a two-year extension, non-auction. Path-clearing for Eutelsat OneWeb, Starlink and Jio Satellite; Amazon Leo still awaiting a GMPCS licence. Cabinet ratification plus security and FDI clearance still required. Why it matters: the world’s largest remaining closed consumer satcom market is moving from licence-theory to a priced assignment, with security clearance now the binding gate. https://www.business-standard.com/industry/news/trai-clears-the-decks-for-satellite-broadband-services-in-india-126090801317_1.html https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/telecom/satcom-spectrum-dcc-clears-trai-recommendations-starlink-oneweb-still-await-security-clearance-for-commercial-launch-14025292.html
