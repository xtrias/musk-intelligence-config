## 1. Orbital launch and space transportation

- **CONFIRMED / operations + competitors.** ISRO’s GSLV Mk II (F17) lifted off at 21:25 UTC on 3 September (02:55 IST on 4 September) from the Second Launch Pad at Satish Dhawan and successfully deployed EOS-05 / GISAT-1A (about 2,367 kg) to a sub-geosynchronous transfer orbit. It is ISRO’s first launch since the January PSLV failure and India’s first imaging satellite destined for geosynchronous orbit; the spacecraft will raise itself to GEO. Why it matters: a government-commercial GTO vehicle is back in service after an eight-month drought, and persistent GEO imaging is a payload class LEO constellations do not replace. [SpaceNews, 3 Sep](https://spacenews.com/gslv-launches-earth-imaging-satellite-in-first-isro-launch-since-january/); [India Today, 3 Sep](https://www.indiatoday.in/science/story/gslv-f17-eos-05-launch-isro-end-launch-drought-geosynchronous-imaging-satellite-2985983-2026-09-03).
- **CONFIRMED / demand + competitors.** ESA Director General Josef Aschbacher told Reuters Europe may raise Ariane 6 and Vega-C cadence because demand — EU missions plus Amazon’s 18 booked Ariane flights plus other commercial work — exceeds planned capacity. Arianespace currently projects 9–10 Ariane 6 flights a year from 2027; a decision is needed soon to hit a ~2030 demand peak. Why it matters: European independent access is now a binding constraint on Amazon Leo and on ESA’s own satellite programs, not a political slogan. [Reuters, 2 Sep, circulating in-window](https://www.reuters.com/science/europe-may-accelerate-space-launches-meet-satellite-demand-2026-09-02/).
- **CONFIRMED / competitors + supply chain.** Sierra Space said it has booked undisclosed national-security and commercial payloads onto Dream Chaser’s inaugural flight alongside the NASA CRS manifest. Launch date remains TBD; the vehicle is queued behind ULA’s Vulcan backlog. Why it matters: runway-return cargo is a third logistics mode versus Dragon and Cygnus, but the ride still depends on a grounded/constrained Vulcan. [Orlando Sentinel, 3 Sep](https://www.orlandosentinel.com/2026/09/03/sierra-space-lines-up-dream-chaser-customers-but-launch-date-still-tbd/).
- **CONFIRMED / regulation (no delta).** FAA/AST posted no new Part 450 license modification in-window after checking the Starship stakeholder page and Spaceflight Now. New Glenn remains grounded after the 28 May LC-36 pad explosion; Vulcan LV-01 remains NET September pending GEM-63XL / BE-4 valve closeout. Next US commercial medium-lift print on the public schedule is Falcon 9 Starlink 15-24, delayed to 6 September from Vandenberg.

Watch next: Isar Aerospace Spectrum Flight 2 (“Onward and Upward”) window opening 4 September 20:00 UTC from Andøya, Norway (Norwegian maritime notices 4–10 September); ArianeGroup capacity briefing 7 September; ULA Vulcan LV-01 and Blue Origin New Glenn dated return-to-flight.
