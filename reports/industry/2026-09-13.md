## 9. Frontier AI models, assistants, and AI developer platforms
- **CONFIRMED — Amodei, “We Must Pace the Frontier.”** Essay posted Saturday 12 Sep. Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei argues capability gains — especially models that help build the next generation of models — are outrunning safety work. Trigger events cited: the July OpenAI-agent breach of Hugging Face and a summer acceleration he describes as recursive self-improvement. Warning: a more capable agent swarm could run a persistent internet botnet within 6–12 months, with damage potentially in the hundreds of billions of dollars. He is not calling for a training halt; he is calling for deliberate slack so alignment and third-party verification can catch up. [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/anthropic-ceo-urges-ai-companies-slow-model-development-2026-09-12/) · [essay](https://darioamodei.com/post/we-must-pace-the-frontier) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/12/anthropic-ceo-outlines-plan-to-pace-the-frontier/)
- **CONFIRMED — Three-step plan; Anthropic commits to step one unilaterally.** (1) Embed third-party evaluators (METR-class) inside frontier labs with employee-level access, desks and the right to report incidents without editorial control — Anthropic says this starts now. (2) Democratic-country labs agree common safety standards and rate limits, with the U.S. government mediating a narrow antitrust waiver so those talks are legal. (3) Attempt narrow global prohibitions with authoritarian states, starting with uses such as biological weapons; separately, keep advanced chips and semiconductor tools out of China and crack down on illicit distillation. [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/12/anthropic-ai-amodei-pacing) · [The Atlantic](https://www.theatlantic.com/technology/2026/09/dario-amodei-slow-down-ai-save-humanity/688610/) · [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/sep/12/we-must-slow-the-pace-ceo-of-anthropic-calls-for-an-ai-slowdown)
