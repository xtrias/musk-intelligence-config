## 2. Satellite broadband and direct-to-device connectivity
- **REPORTED / regulation + competitors + technology.** A 7 September 24/7 Wall St. industry note framed the D2D contest as a spectrum contest rather than a launch contest: AST SpaceMobile cited with 60-plus MNO partners and a coverage claim of about 3 billion subscribers, targeting roughly 100 MHz of US spectrum, Q2 revenue $31.5 million, FY2026 guide $150–200 million and a $1.3 billion backlog, with beta still this year; Starlink Mobile V2 described as holding 65 MHz nationwide after the EchoStar transfer (15 MHz AWS-3 + 40 MHz AWS-4 + 10 MHz H-Block). Why it matters: once launch cadence is no longer the scarce input, licensing and terrestrial-partner spectrum set who can actually complete a phone call. Treat the subscriber and MHz figures as the outlet’s compilation, not a new FCC grant. Why it matters: the world’s largest BESS-cell producer is applying the same overcapacity brake already used in solar and EV manufacturing, even as grid-side demand and CATL TENER Sodium first named China deliveries are still scheduled this month. No CATL, BYD Energy or Fluence plant was named as paused. Still needs Ipswich works approval, financial close, and a Tesla EPC + LTSA. The same precinct already hosts CleanCo’s 250 MW / 500 MWh Swanbank BESS (opened February 2026). Why it matters: former coal nodes are becoming BESS hubs; EPBC queue speed is now a binding constraint. [Energy-Storage.News, 8 Sep](https://www.energy-storage.news/octopus-australia-refers-2gwh-blackstone-bess-for-epbc-act-assessment-in-queensland/).

[Energy-Storage.News, 7 Sep](https://www.energy-storage.news/macquarie-backed-blueleaf-energy-and-universal-peak-close-financing-on-malaysia-tender-winning-bess-project/).

Why it matters: a large South Asian buyer is writing storage into the RFP rather than treating it as optional.
