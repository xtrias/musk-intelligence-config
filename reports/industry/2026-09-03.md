## 1. Orbital launch and space transportation

- **CONFIRMED / operations + competitors.** Rocket Lab’s Electron “Owl Around The World” lifted off at 12:01 UTC on 2 September from Mahia LC-1 and deployed a Synspective StriX SAR satellite to 575 km LEO. It was Electron’s 94th flight, the 15th of 2026 and the 11th dedicated Synspective mission, with 16 more booked through 2030. Why it matters: small-lift dedicated cadence is the commercial alternative to Falcon rideshare while medium/heavy peers New Glenn and Vulcan remain grounded. [Rocket Lab / GlobeNewswire, 2 Sep](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/02/3355246/0/en/mission-success-rocket-lab-launches-94th-electron-mission.html); [Space.com, 2 Sep](https://www.space.com/space-exploration/launches-spacecraft/watch-rocket-lab-launch-japanese-radar-satellite-to-orbit-early-aug-31).

Booster B1063 flew its 35th mission and landed on the droneship *Of Course I Still Love You*. Space.com put the flight as the 102nd Falcon 9 of 2026. Why it matters: West Coast Falcon cadence is still the only US medium-lift line flying at weekly tempo. [SpaceX mission page](https://www.spacex.com/launches/sl-15-23); [Space.com, 2 Sep](https://www.space.com/space-exploration/launches-spacecraft/spacex-falcon-9-starlink-group-15-23-launch-ocisly).

Why it matters: an environmental-license barrier comes down before any higher-cadence or orbital Starship profile. [FAA Starship stakeholder page](https://www.faa.gov/space/stakeholder_engagement/spacex_starship); [Aviation Week, 2 Sep](https://aviationweek.com/space/operations-safety/faa-authorizes-wider-swath-starship-ops).

Why it matters: Blue Origin is being paid for deep-space infrastructure while New Glenn itself remains grounded after the 28 May LC-36 pad explosion, with return-to-flight still targeted before year-end. [SpaceNews, 2 Sep](https://spacenews.com/nasa-selects-blue-origin-for-mars-communications-orbiter/).
- **CONFIRMED / competitors (watch item).** ISRO’s GSLV-F17 / EOS-05 (GISAT-1A) is targeted for 4 September 2026 from Sriharikota SLP — India’s first geosynchronous imaging satellite and a return-to-flight after a seven-month hiatus since the PSLV-C62 failure. Why it matters: government-commercial GEO imaging is a payload class Falcon 9 and LVM3 both chase. [India Today, 2 Sep](https://www.indiatoday.in/science/story/gslv-f17-eos-05-launch-why-isro-is-sending-its-first-imaging-satellite-to-geosynchronous-orbit-2985191-2026-09-02).

Why it matters: Amazon is trying to enter the D2D lane already occupied by AST SpaceMobile (FCC SCS license) and Starlink D2D spectrum transactions. [FCC DA-26-866](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DA-26-866A1.pdf).
- **CONFIRMED / regulation (live clock).** The FCC Amazon–Globalstar transfer docket GN 26-134 remained on a running 180-day clock as of 2 September (clock started 4 June). Why it matters: Globalstar’s MSS licenses are the spectrum backbone of Amazon Leo D2D. [FCC transaction page](https://www.fcc.gov/transaction/amazon-globalstar).
