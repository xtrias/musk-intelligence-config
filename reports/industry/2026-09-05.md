[European Spaceflight, 4 Sep](https://europeanspaceflight.com/isar-aerospace-calls-off-4-september-spectrum-launch-attempt/); [NRK Nordland, 4 Sep](https://www.nrk.no/nordland/avlyser-dagens-oppskytning-pa-andoya-1.18014118).

Why it matters: another LEO breakup in an unusual inclination adds conjunction risk for commercial traffic and underscores passivation gaps. [SpaceNews, 4 Sep](https://spacenews.com/chinese-reconnaissance-satellite-breaks-up-in-rare-retrograde-orbit/).

## 4. Autonomous driving / robotaxi software and regulation

- **CONFIRMED / regulation.** NHTSA opened Audit Query AQ26002 on 3 September and announced it on 4 September into Tesla’s FMVSS self-certification of Cybercab after paid Austin deployment began Thursday. Estimated population: 1,000 vehicles. The cars lack permanently attached conventional manual controls (steering wheel, brake and accelerator pedals, mirrors). NHTSA will examine the process and technical data Tesla relied on, including the extent to which certification depended on determinations that certain FMVSS are inapplicable. Texas records showed 420 autonomous vehicles registered as of Friday morning, including 45 Cybercabs. Tesla did not immediately comment. Administrator Jonathan Morrison: the agency “fully supports the safe development and deployment of automated vehicles” but “need[s] to ensure that all of our laws are followed.” Why it matters: Tesla took the self-certification path rather than the Part 555 exemption Zoox received in July (up to 2,500 vehicles a year for two years). That is the live federal fork for no-controls robotaxis. [NHTSA press, 4 Sep](https://www.nhtsa.gov/press-releases/investigation-tesla-cybercab-self-certification); [NHTSA ODI resume AQ26002](https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/inv/2026/INOA-AQ26002-17078.pdf); [Reuters, 4 Sep](https://www.reuters.com/business/autos-transportation/us-auto-safety-regulator-opens-probe-into-nearly-1000-tesla-cybercabs-2026-09-04/); [NYT, 4 Sep](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/09/04/business/tesla-cybercab-nhtsa-investigation.html).
- **CONFIRMED / regulation.** USDOT released “America Leads: DOT’s National Strategy for Automated Vehicles, Fiscal Years 2026–2030” on 3 September, with legal and trade analysis circulating 4 September. Four goals: safety including first ADS-competency standards; regulatory certainty and FMVSS modernization; commercial passenger and freight; interoperable corridors. The strategy is guidance, not a binding rule; existing FMVSS remain in force unless an exemption is granted. Why it matters: it is the administration’s multi-year map, but it does not itself legalize no-controls vehicles. [JDSupra / Holland & Knight, 4 Sep](https://www.jdsupra.com/legalnews/the-road-ahead-for-driverless-vehicles-5025261/).
- **CONFIRMED / regulation + operations.** The UK launched an Automated Vehicles Act Implementation Programme aimed at safe AV deployment by 2027; Highways News on 4 September also reported the Department for Transport fast-tracking driverless taxi and bus-like pilots from spring 2026 under the APS permitting scheme, with Wayve cited. Why it matters: London’s 3 September Uber–Wayve supervised service now sits under a dated national implementation clock rather than a one-off TfL permit. [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/automated-vehicles-act-implementation-programme); [Highways News, 4 Sep](https://highways-news.com/government-launches-automated-vehicles-act-implementation-programme/).
- No material last-24-hour Reuters or Bloomberg item on Mobileye, Baidu Apollo Go, Pony.ai or WeRide after checks. Waymo’s Denver / San Diego / Tampa paid openings and Zoox’s Houston / San Diego test start remain 1 September events.

Watch next: NHTSA AQ26002 information request and any Tesla response; whether Cybercab stays on Austin streets during the audit; ADS-competency ANPRM timing.

## 5. Grid-scale energy storage and distributed energy

- **CONFIRMED / regulation + operations.** German police investigated a further grid-sabotage attempt on 4 September (Dormagen transformer) after earlier attacks on an RWE-linked Bergheim substation that took about 4,200 MW of generation offline and a 50Hertz node in Brandenburg. Why it matters: this is a resilience and insurance story for BESS and interconnectors, not a storage-order print. [Reuters, 4 Sep](https://www.reuters.com/world/german-police-investigate-new-grid-sabotage-attempt-bild-reports-2026-09-04/); [Bloomberg, 4 Sep](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-04/german-police-probe-fresh-attempts-to-sabotage-power-grid).
- **CONFIRMED / demand + supply chain.** CNESA data, published on the Energy-Storage.News desk on 4 September: China commissioned 21.81 GW / 58.60 GWh of new-type storage in H1 2026, down 18 percent in power and 16 percent in energy year-on-year — the first recorded H1 decline. Newly commissioned project count fell 51 percent. Chinese firms booked 298 GWh of overseas orders in H1, up 83 percent. Cumulative new-type capacity: 168.3 GW / 448.7 GWh. Why it matters: domestic demand is rotating from project count to larger, longer-duration systems while export order books absorb the slack. [Energy-Storage.News, 4 Sep](https://www.energy-storage.news/chinas-half-year-energy-storage-deployments-post-first-ever-decline-to-22gw-59gwh-while-overseas-orders-surge-83/).
- **CONFIRMED / capital + supply chain.** Flex agreed to buy EPC Power from Goldman Sachs Alternatives and Cleanhill Partners for $4.4 billion (agreement 3 September, expected close Q4). EPC makes software-defined inverters, power-conversion systems, microgrid and data-center power hardware including solid-state transformers. Why it matters: PCS is a small share of BESS capex but a large share of opex and integrator reputation when it fails; the buyer is a contract manufacturer already inside data-center power. [Energy-Storage.News, 4 Sep](https://www.energy-storage.news/goldman-sachs-alternatives-cleanhill-partners-sell-pcs-manufacturer-epc-power-for-us4-4-billion/).
- **CONFIRMED / operations + demand.** Equinor-owned East Point Energy began commercial operations at Citrus Flatts, a 100 MW / 200 MWh merchant BESS in Harlingen, Cameron County, Texas, on the ERCOT system. Danske Commodities will optimize. Combined with Sunset Ridge, Equinor now has 110 MW / 220 MWh in Texas. Why it matters: a European major is running merchant ERCOT storage as an independent power producer, not a utility rate-base asset. [Energy-Storage.News, 4 Sep](https://www.energy-storage.news/equinors-east-point-energy-begins-operations-at-200mwh-texas-bess/).

Watch next: CATL TENER Sodium first China customer deliveries scheduled this month under the HyperStrong 60 GWh deal; first named Fluence hyperscale / data-center BESS booking; German inertia-prequalified inverters.
