### Tesla — France opens supervised FSD road tests
**CONFIRMED.** Reuters (Paris, 3 Sep) and French outlets (01net, Le Parisien, Auto Journal, RMC): after a videoconference with Musk late 2 Sep, Transport Minister Philippe Tabarot said France will put two Tesla FSD vehicles on public roads. July he had publicly refused FSD over speed-margin and driver-attention concerns. Today’s framing: “constructive exchange,” months of technical adaptations, then “a new phase: that of on-road testing.” Ministry source to Reuters: tests are to verify Dutch and Tesla data on French roads; results targeted mid-to-late September so France can vote at the EU Technical Committee on Motor Vehicles meeting on 6 Oct (or, if not then, early December). Qualified majority: 15 of 27 states representing 65% of population. Already supporting: Netherlands, Belgium, Denmark, Estonia, Lithuania. Tesla had no immediate comment. Supervised FSD, not unsupervised robotaxi. https://live.euronext.com/en/financial-news/france-starts-tests-teslas-self-driving-tech

### Tesla — Autopilot litigation and a newly matched fatality file
**REPORTED.** Electrek, 2 Sep (still the only named-outlet write-up; no new terms on 3 Sep): Tesla confidentially settled *Caleb Mendoza et al. v. Tesla, Inc.* (N.D. Cal. 24-cv-08738-VC, Judge Vince Chhabria). 18 Feb 2023, I-680 Contra Costa County: 2014 Model S on Autopilot, about 70 mph, struck a parked county ladder truck; driver Genesis Giovanni Mendoza Martinez, 31, killed; brother Caleb Mendoza injured. A May 2025 ruling had kept fraudulent-misrepresentation claims alive, including that the Autopilot name was “plausibly misleading.” Amount undisclosed; the settlement avoids an October jury trial that would have put Musk’s Autopilot statements in front of jurors. Fred Lambert’s running estimate of Autopilot/FSD legal exposure (up to $14 billion in cases he tracks) is an advocate figure, not a court finding. https://electrek.co/2026/09/02/tesla-settles-autopilot-fire-truck-death-lawsuit/
