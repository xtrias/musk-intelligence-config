### Orbital launch and space transportation
**CONFIRMED.** SpaceX flew Starlink Group 15-27 from Vandenberg SLC-4E at 18:47 PDT Saturday / 01:47 UTC Sunday (03:47 CEST). Falcon 9 booster B1093 flew its 17th mission with 27 Starlink V2 Mini satellites and landed on the droneship *Of Course I Still Love You* in the Pacific about 8.5 minutes after liftoff. Deployment was confirmed about 62 minutes after launch. SpaceX said the mission marked the first 40th flight of a fairing half. Space.com (Michael Wall, 20 September) called it the 107th Falcon 9 mission of 2026 and the 81st Starlink mission of the year, and cited Jonathan McDowell for more than 11,100 active Starlink spacecraft before this batch. NSF/Facebook recaps circulating overnight used different year-to-date tallies (including a 690th Falcon-family / 111th SpaceX-2026 count). Those figures conflict across outlets; the company itself did not publish a cumulative tally in the launch posts. Operationally this is another West Coast Starlink cadence flight while Florida pads are held for Starship and crew work. ([Space.com](https://www.space.com/space-exploration/launches-spacecraft/spacex-starlink-launch-group-15-27-landing-ocisly); [Spaceflight Now tag](https://spaceflightnow.com/tag/spacex/); [SpaceX launch](https://x.com/SpaceX/status/2101485793294389629); [deployment](https://x.com/SpaceX/status/2101504221266792661); [fairing](https://x.com/SpaceX/status/2101489521615385011))
