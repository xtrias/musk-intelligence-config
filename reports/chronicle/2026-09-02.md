### Orbital launch — Falcon 9 Starlink 15-23
**CONFIRMED.** SpaceX flew Falcon 9 from Vandenberg SLC-4E at 1:42 a.m. PT (08:42 UTC) on 2 Sep with 27 Starlink satellites. ### Starship Flight 14 paperwork
**REPORTED / FILING.** Coverage circulating today of FCC experimental and Starlink-spectrum filings describes Flight 14 as the first Starship profile with an “orbital second stage,” a requested radio window from 15 Sep 2026, and an intended operational deploy of next-generation Starlink V3 satellites rather than a same-day suborbital demo. STA 1597-EX-ST-2026 (granted 12 Aug) covers Flight 14 launch-vehicle comms from Boca Chica. This is communications authority, not an FAA launch license and not an official T-0. CADENA/COMPASS planning notes and secondary recaps have used NET 15 Sep; SpaceX has not posted a public countdown. ### Tesla — Autopilot litigation and a tiny U.S. recall
**REPORTED.** Electrek, 2 Sep: Tesla confidentially settled Caleb Mendoza et al. v. Tesla (N.D. Cal. 24-cv-08738-VC, Judge Vince Chhabria), a wrongful-death case from an 18 Feb 2023 I-680 Contra Costa crash in which a 2014 Model S on Autopilot struck a parked fire truck at about 70 mph, killing driver Genesis Mendoza, 31, and injuring his brother. A May 2025 ruling had kept fraudulent-misrepresentation claims alive, including that the Autopilot name was “plausibly misleading.” Amount undisclosed; the settlement avoids a jury trial. https://electrek.co/2026/09/02/tesla-settles-autopilot-fire-truck-death-lawsuit/

**CONFIRMED / FILING.** NHTSA recall 26V558 (manufacturer report 31 Aug; acknowledgement circulating 1–2 Sep): 19 Model Year 2026 Model Ys built 26 Jul–4 Aug 2026. Front-suspension lateral-link-to-subframe fasteners may not have been torqued after a PLC used in a recovery procedure was inadvertently disabled. Risk: link loosen or separate, alignment shift, loss of control. Tesla: one related warranty/field report as of 26 Aug; no known crashes, injuries or deaths. Remedy at Service; owner letters planned. Distinct from Chinese Model Y L rear-spring sag reports (owner social media, no regulator file). Drive Tesla Canada recap: https://driveteslacanada.ca/?p=120353

**REPORTED.** Electrek 1 Sep matched a redacted NHTSA Standing General Order filing to a 25 Mar 2026 Batavia, Illinois Model Y left-turn crash that killed passenger Maggie Espinosa, 37. Tesla’s filing: automation “Verified Engaged,” 24 mph left turn. Software version and ODD redacted.

**REPORTED.** Electrek / Teslarati 2 Sep: first-batch China Model Y L owners report rear-suspension sag. Service threshold cited by owners: replace springs if rear arch height falls below 764 mm (nominal ~784 mm). Not a recall.
