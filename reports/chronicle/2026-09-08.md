### Autonomy / robotaxi — Slovenia FSD Supervised (CONFIRMED, Reuters 8 Sep)
Reuters, datelined Stockholm 8 September 07:18 UTC, reported that Slovenia has allowed Tesla’s FSD (Supervised) driver-assistance system on its roads, becoming the sixth European country to do so ahead of a possible EU-wide vote. The underlying Tesla Europe post went up Monday 7 September 17:00 UTC: “FSD Supervised now approved in Slovenia. Rollout will begin soon.” Slovenia’s infrastructure and energy minister Jernej Vrtovec reposted it as “Developing Slovenia.”

The primary Slovenian document is a 8 September GOV.SI notice from the Ministry of Infrastructure and Energy. Javna agencija Republike Slovenije za varnost prometa (AVP) recognized the temporary EU type-approval of FSD (Supervised) issued by the Dutch RDW on 10 April 2026 under Regulation (EU) 2018/858. Recognition is mutual-recognition of a temporary new-technology approval, not a new Slovenian test campaign. The driver remains responsible. Prior national clearances: Netherlands (April), Lithuania, Estonia, Denmark, Belgium. Reuters added that Finland and Greece are considering approvals and that France, after July safety objections, last week began testing two FSD-equipped cars. Tesla has said an EU Technical Committee on Motor Vehicles vote could come as soon as 6 October; that vote, not Slovenia, is the prize.

### Autonomy / robotaxi — Cybercab virtual joystick + factory Starlink hatch (REPORTED / SIGHTING)
Electrek, The Verge and InsideEVs on 8 September wrote up an Austin Cybercab ride on 7 September in which rider Matthew Skrzypczak found the cabin screen in a service layout: an on-screen virtual joystick with forward/reverse, stop, horn and door controls instead of the rider UI. Tesla has not commented. The outlets treat it as leftover employee/depot mode for low-speed repositioning of a vehicle that has no steering wheel or pedals. Operationally it matters because NHTSA Open Audit Query AQ26002 (opened 3 September; estimated population about 1,000 Cybercabs) is an Office of Vehicle Safety Compliance audit of Tesla’s self-certification of a vehicle lacking permanently attached conventional manual controls. A touchscreen joystick is a form of manual control Tesla has not shown publicly. The audit is not a crash investigation and is not a stop-sale.
