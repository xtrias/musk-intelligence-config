### Orbital launch and space transportation
**CONFIRMED.** NASA awarded SpaceX a firm-fixed-price CCtCap modification worth $946 million for Crew-15, Crew-16 and Crew-17. The change takes SpaceX to 17 missions under the contract and lifts the total CCtCap value with SpaceX to $5.92 billion, with performance through 2030. The package covers ground, launch, in-orbit and return/recovery operations, cargo on each flight, and a docked lifeboat at ISS. NASA said the sole-source modification does not block later buys from a second provider. Crew-12 is currently docked. NASA framed the award as keeping two U.S. commercial crew options; CNBC noted Boeing’s Starliner is still targeting an uncrewed flight in the coming months and crewed flights in Q3 2027. ([NASA](https://www.nasa.gov/missions/station/commercial-crew/nasa-awards-spacex-three-crew-flights-to-space-station/); [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/spacex-fly-more-nasa-crews-space-station-under-expanded-592-billion-deal-2026-09-18/))

**CONFIRMED.** SpaceX moved Starship Flight 14 from NET Tuesday 22 September to NET Monday 28 September, “pending regulatory approval.” The company gave no public reason. The mission page still describes a ~75-minute window opening 7:15 a.m. Central from Starbase OLP-2: first planned orbital insertion (~275 km, about six orbits, nearly 10 hours), deployment of 26 operational Starlink V3 satellites (1 Tbps each; 26 Tbps claimed on this flight), Super Heavy splashdown in the Gulf, ship splashdown west of Chile. No Mechazilla catch. Aviation Week’s Irene Klotz reported the slip against still-pending FCC approval. Yahoo Finance reported SPCX down more than 2% Friday on the delay; Barron’s/JPMorgan figures cited there (~$570 million retail selling over three weeks) are bank commentary, not a company filing. ([SpaceX mission page](https://www.spacex.com/launches/starship-flight-14); [SpaceX post](https://x.com/SpaceX/status/2100633608734081213); [Aviation Week](https://aviationweek.com/space/commercial-space/spacex-retargets-starship-flight-sept-28); [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/article/spacex-stock-falls-as-starships-test-flight-slips-to-sept-28-retail-investors-sell-shares-143200705.html))
