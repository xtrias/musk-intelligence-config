### Frontier AI models, assistants, and AI infrastructure
**CONFIRMED (Friday product; Saturday secondary coverage).** SpaceXAI released Grok Voice Transcribe 2.0 on Friday via the company blog and @SpaceXAI. Company claims: roughly twice the accuracy of Transcribe 1.0 at the same price; first among 32 streaming models on the public Artificial Analysis leaderboard; streaming final-transcript WER 2.7% at 0.49 seconds after end of speech; non-streaming AA-WER 2.3% (from 4.0% on 1.0); internal short-phrase multilingual WER 20.6% → 6.8%. List price unchanged: $0.10 per hour batch, $0.20 per hour streaming, with diarization, timestamps and key-term biasing included. Atlassian said Loom is switching transcripts onto 2.0 and piping them into Cursor. SpaceXAI said 2.0 will become the Speech-to-Text API default and 1.0 will be deprecated in coming weeks unless callers pin `grok-voice-transcribe-1.0`. The Next Web correctly flagged that “twice as accurate” is versus the prior xAI model, not versus every rival. The same audio stack is what Neuralink captioned on the Terry VOICE video. ([xAI blog](https://x.ai/news/grok-voice-transcribe-2); [SpaceXAI](https://x.com/SpaceXAI/status/2101005248311726387))

**REPORTED.** Associated Press / Politico / CBS / Bloomberg Law / Los Angeles Times, Friday night–Saturday: *Buist v. Anthropic PBC*, N.D. Cal. No. 3:26-cv-10693, filed 18 September. Four named paid subscribers to ChatGPT, Claude, Grok or Gemini allege that Anthropic, OpenAI, SpaceXAI and Google violated Sherman Act §1 by coordinating a slowdown after Dario Amodei’s 12 September essay “We Must Pace the Frontier” and same-day public agreement from Sam Altman, Elon Musk and Demis Hassabis. Plaintiffs’ theory: an agreement to improve frontier models more slowly than competition would otherwise produce reduces the value of paid subscriptions. Counsel: Trial Lawyers for Justice; Nick Rowley quoted. The filing is a complaint, not a finding. None of the defendants had a same-day named-outlet response in this pull. ([Politico](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/09/18/anthropic-openai-spacexai-google-sued-over-calls-to-pace-ai-development-01085023); [CBS/AP](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/ai-slowdown-lawsuit-openai-anthropic-google/); [Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/litigation/openai-anthropic-google-spacexai-hit-with-antitrust-lawsuit))
