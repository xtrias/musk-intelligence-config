### SpaceX — Falcon 9 Starlink 15-24 from Vandenberg
**CONFIRMED.** SpaceX launched 27 Starlink satellites from Space Launch Complex 4E at Vandenberg Space Force Base on Sunday 6 Sep at 10:26 a.m. EDT / 7:26 a.m. PDT / 1426 UTC. First stage booster **B1088**, flying for the **19th** time, landed about 8.5 minutes after liftoff on the droneship *Of Course I Still Love You* in the Pacific. The upper stage deployed the 27 satellites on schedule 62 minutes after launch. Official @SpaceX confirmed deployment and posted launch photos.

Space.com (Michael Wall, 6 Sep) called it the **103rd Falcon 9 launch of 2026**. Spaceflight Now’s 5 Sep live page separately called the same mission the **80th Starlink flight of 2026**. Do not merge those two counts. B1088 is roughly halfway to the company reuse record of 37 flights, set by B1067 on 25 Aug. KeepTrack’s 6 Sep brief put the constellation at 12,881 satellites launched, 11,093 in orbit, 11,078 operational — treat those tracker figures as **REPORTED**, not a company release.

Primary: [SpaceX launch post](https://x.com/SpaceX/status/2096625304009597207); [deployment confirmation](https://x.com/SpaceX/status/2096621981328130462); [Space.com](https://www.space.com/space-exploration/launches-spacecraft/spacex-starlink/group-15-24-launch-ocisly); [Spaceflight Now live page](https://spaceflightnow.com/2026/09/05/live-coverage-spacex-to-launch-80th-starlink-mission-of-2026/).

**REPORTED operational shift.** Space.com and KeepTrack both state that Falcon 9 Starlink missions now fly exclusively from Vandenberg, with Florida Starlink cadence moving to Starship. That matches SpaceX launch VP Kiko Dontchev’s earlier confirmation (outside this window) and the 25 Aug Florida Starlink 10-49 flight listed as the last F9 Starlink from the Cape.

### Tesla — FSD safety layer, 24/7 timeline, Cybercab aftershock
U.S. markets were closed Sunday. Last print remains Friday 4 Sep: **TSLA $354.08, −5.92%**, volume ~65 million (StatMuse / MarketWatch). That drop followed the 3 Sep invite-only Cybercab event and the 4 Sep NHTSA audit; it is context for Sunday commentary, not a new session.

**CONFIRMED software (announced Fri, still rolling Sunday).** Tesla AI said FSD Supervised **v14.3.9** adds an active-safety layer that can engage FSD on the driver’s behalf when an imminent collision is detected and Automatic Emergency Braking may not be enough, and may also engage if the cabin camera flags heavy distraction or an accidental FSD disengagement. Electrek (4 Sep) and Teslarati (5 Sep, in-window) both treat it as a background collision-evasion net during manual driving, not unsupervised autonomy. Not a Tesla App: the feature requires FSD to be enabled/paid; after an intervention the car is expected to hand control back or pull over if the driver is inattentive.

[Teslarati](https://www.teslarati.com/tesla-full-self-driving-overtake-manual-driving-avoid-disaster/); [Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/09/04/tesla-fsd-active-safety-collision-avoidance/)

### xAI / Grok
**CONFIRMED court order (Friday 4 Sep; Sunday recaps).** U.S. District Judge Donovan Frank (D. Minn.) denied xAI’s motion for a preliminary injunction against Minnesota’s HF 1606 “nudification” ban (Minn. Stat. § 325E.91). The law, in effect since 1 Aug, bars operators from allowing users to generate AI nudes of identifiable people and carries civil penalties up to $500,000 per violation. Frank found xAI had not shown irreparable harm sufficient to pause enforcement while First Amendment claims proceed; he wrote the balance of harms “tips steeply” toward the state. xAI said in a Friday filing it will appeal to the 8th Circuit. Deccan Chronicle and other Sunday wires restated the order; the underlying docket entry is 4 Sep.

[Star Tribune](https://www.startribune.com/judge-keeps-minnesota-nudification-law-in-effect-over-challenge-from-elon-musks-ai-company/601886350); [MPR](https://www.mprnews.org/story/2026/09/04/judge-lets-minnesota-enforce-antinudification-app-law-over-xai-objection-as-case-proceeds); docket X.AI LLC v. Ellison, D. Minn. 0:26-cv-03425 (order filed 4 Sep).
