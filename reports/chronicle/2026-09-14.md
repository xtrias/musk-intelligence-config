## NEWS DESK (web + filings first)

### Orbital launch — SpaceX Falcon 700 / SES O3b
**CONFIRMED.** On Sunday 13 September at 14:49 ET (18:49 UTC), a Falcon 9 lifted off from SLC-40 at Cape Canaveral Space Force Station with the final three Boeing-built SES O3b mPOWER satellites (F11–F13). SpaceX called it the 700th mission of the Falcon family (Falcon 1 + Falcon 9 + Falcon Heavy). First-stage booster B1080 completed its 29th flight and landed on droneship *A Shortfall of Gravitas*. SpaceX confirmed deployment of all three spacecraft into medium Earth orbit. Space.com counted it as the 105th Falcon 9 launch of 2026. The flight finishes SES’s initial 13-satellite mPOWER constellation, lofted entirely on Falcon 9 since December 2022. Operationally this is a commercial MEO broadband mission for SES, not a Starlink flight; Florida Starlink Falcon 9s have been shifted toward Vandenberg while Cape pads absorb other traffic and LC-39A Starship-tower work. Sources: SpaceX posts; Space.com; Spaceflight Now; Florida Today; Orlando Sentinel; SES/Business Wire.

### Autonomy / social-ads legal — X Corp and SpaceXAI drop Apple, keep OpenAI
**CONFIRMED.** Reuters, CNBC and Bloomberg reported Monday that X Corp and SpaceXAI (the SpaceX AI division that now houses Grok) moved in the Northern District of Texas to dismiss with prejudice their antitrust claims against Apple. The same filing states they are **not** dismissing claims against OpenAI. Case: *X Corp. et al. v. Apple Inc. et al.*, 4:25-cv-00914. CourtListener has the motion (ECF 385). The complaint had alleged Apple unlawfully locked ChatGPT into Apple Intelligence and shut out rival chatbots. The filing does not describe settlement terms; several outlets used “settle” anyway. Apple, OpenAI and Musk’s companies did not comment to Reuters. Why it matters: it ends the iPhone-distribution fight while leaving the chatbot-monopoly fight against OpenAI alive. Sources: Reuters; CNBC; Bloomberg; CourtListener.

### Autonomy / insurance — Zurich Australia prices FSD Supervised
