### Satellite broadband — Argentina rural-school kits signed (CONFIRMED, 8 Sep evening)
The Argentine government and Starlink posted the same night that Capital Humano, ENACOM and Starlink Argentina S.R.L. signed the school-connectivity agreement. Official copy: Starlink will install 6,000 kits for rural public schools and isolated communities under the project “Conectividad Satelital en Establecimientos Educativos de Gestión Pública de la República Argentina,” inside ENACOM’s public-interest connectivity program. Capital Humano selects the schools and coordinates with provinces; ENACOM supplies the regulatory frame and Fondo del Servicio Universal money.

The legal architecture is older than the signing photo. ENACOM Resolución 857/2026, published in the Boletín Oficial on 2 September, already approved a donation-with-charge: Starlink Argentina valued at USD 9,978,000 (USD 2.778 million for 6,000 kits/accessories/shipping plus USD 7.2 million for 24 months of service), paid in four tranches into the FSU. Public funds of USD 11,898,000 cover installation, service management, technical support, a monitoring API, and 12 extra months of service after Starlink’s two years end. Program total: USD 21,876,000. La Nación and Perfil reported those figures when the resolution posted; today’s fact is the ministerial signing and the official @Starlink product post (8 Sep 23:55 UTC).

Operationally this is a 6,000-terminal education deployment with a two-year prepaid service block, not a consumer-ARPU print. Installation is specified to run through authorized Argentine distributors.

### Satellite / spectrum — FCC tees September votes; constellation NEPA practice (REPORTED, 8 Sep)

### Autonomy / robotaxi — FSD v14.3.9 Automatic Collision Evasion ships; Cybercab still under AQ26002 (CONFIRMED / REPORTED)

The new HW4-only feature is Automatic Collision Evasion. Release notes: the system can activate FSD (Supervised) while the driver is in manual control, then keep driving, in two cases. (1) Imminent frontal collision with another vehicle on a controlled-access highway below 85 mph, no vulnerable road users in path, road not detected wet/slippery, braking alone may not suffice — the car may steer around onto a shoulder or open lane. ### AI infrastructure — Scientific American on Colossus turbines (REPORTED, 9 Sep)

MDEQ counts cited: 27 turbines acknowledged in January 2026, 46 in May, 60 in June, 69 by July — about 1,775 MW of temporary machines.
